Problem Statement 

The company faces the following database requirements:

1. Every customer and product must have a unique identification number generated automatically.

2. Customer email addresses must be unique.

3. Customer Information Validation: Customer names and email addresses must be mandatory 

3. Important fields must not accept NULL values.

4. Order status, payment status,stock quantity, and other fields should have default values.

5. Orders must be linked to valid customer
 and order items must be linked to valid orders and products.

6. The total price of each order item must be calculated automatically based on quantity and unit price.
7. The company wants to demonstrate the use of the MD5() function to generate a hash value.

8. Customer Identification: Every customer must have a unique identification number generated automatically using the AUTO_INCREMENT attribute. 

9. Customer Information Validation: Customer names and email addresses must be mandatory
   
10. Default Values: The database must automatically assign 
    appropriate default values for product prices, stock quantities, order status, payment status, order dates, and order-item quantities wherever applicable.
